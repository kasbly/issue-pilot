DIMENSION: a11y — usable by keyboard and screen reader, readable by everyone.

Hunt for: interactive elements without accessible names (icon-only buttons, unlabeled
inputs); controls unreachable or untrappable by keyboard; missing focus states;
click handlers on divs instead of buttons/links; images without alt text where the
image carries meaning; color as the only signal (status dots, error highlighting);
contrast failures on text and controls; modals that don't trap and restore focus.

Method: read component code as if you cannot see the screen and cannot use a mouse.
Forms, tables with actions, and custom widgets (dropdowns, dialogs, tabs) are where
the bodies are buried.

Every issue: the affected control, who it locks out, file:line, and the fix (usually
a semantic element, a label, or a focus handler — small diffs, big impact).
